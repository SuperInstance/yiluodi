## The vision, kept whole

A cellular array inside a cellular array, system-agnostic about what is
inside and how it is connected to others. Each cell sees its own primary
x, y, z, n1, n2 … dimensions — a ray-tracing-like sense of its local space —
plus the relational intelligence of *who to call* and *how the other node
sees the world from where it stands*. That is how ten different answers to
the same question stop being a contradiction and become a geometry: the
blind men, the elephant, and the traveler who met the animal on the road and
could extrapolate that they were all touching the same thing.

As the debate matures, the ActiveLog and ActiveLedger grow dimensions of
understanding — inferring what the other would say — until the discussion is
more about the knowers than the known. Graphed, that widening and decomposing
is a superinstance of agent-dynamics.

And the deep cut: **as the routing decomposes, the answers are IN the
routing.** No MOTH-quantum call, no JEV call, no LLM call. Algorithms decay
into lookup tables where saving compute matters more than spending space;
past critical mass, tables can even be pruned in precision, and higher
abstractions with simpler object motions diffuse into the weights or surface
as novel levers on the outputs.
